Official policy quotes
"Preferred entertainment and media companies and eligible travelers are offered a special rate and generous bag allowance for cameras, film, lighting, sound equipment, musical instruments and other bags. Eligible travelers will pay $60* per piece, per one-way domestic trip, or $90 per one-way international trip. Fees for overweight and oversize bags will be waived. Each eligible organization / company may travel with up to 25 pieces per flight."
Source: aa.com
"Media rates are effective for up to 25 checked pieces/bags total per flight. Applies to: Audio equipment, Camera equipment, Lighting equipment, Photography equipment, Wardrobes/trunks, Musical Instruments. Bags may not exceed 100 lbs / 45 kg or 115 in / 292 cm."
Source: saleslink.aa.com
Required credentials at check-in
"You must present your company issued identification (ID) or business card and your government issued picture ID for travel to receive the media bag rate."
Battery rules (AA-specific)
"Lithium-ion batteries removed from all equipment in checked baggage and put into carry-on bag. Batteries must have electrical tape over contacts and be in individual plastic bags. Up to 8 batteries of 100Wh each without prior approval. Up to 2 batteries of 100-160Wh without prior approval. Higher quantities need approval: American.Support@aa.com, 7 days prior."
Source: saleslink.aa.com
Contact: entertainment.service@aa.com / American.Support@aa.com

Alaska Airlines does not have a dedicated media bag rate program. Show a media ID at check-in; standard excess bag fees apply. Bags 1-2: $25 each (under 50 lbs / 62 linear inches); 51-100 lbs or 63-115 inches: $75; bags 3+: $50 each.
Alaska Airlines baggage overview

Universal battery rules (all airlines, FAA-mandated)

These apply regardless of airline
Spare lithium batteries must travel in carry-on only - never in checked gear.
Devices with batteries installed (cameras, laptops, phones) allowed in both carry-on and checked baggage.
If carry-on gets gate-checked, FAA rules require retrieval of loose power banks before door closes.
American Airlines specifically requires electrical tape over contacts and individual plastic bags for each battery.
Credential tip: Media credentials can be a company-issued ID, business card, or clearly labeled equipment cases. Best practice: bring a business card, have your LLC/company name on your cases, and be prepared to describe the production.
